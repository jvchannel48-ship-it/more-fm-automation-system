# RJ FM — No. 1 Bicol Region schedule

All times are Manila time (UTC+8). RJ FM broadcasts from the ABS-CBN Complex in Naga City.

## Afternoon DZMM window

- **Monday–Saturday, 2:00 PM–3:00 PM:** DZMM live. The handoff window runs through 3:00:59 PM, with RJ FM music automatically resuming at **3:01 PM**.
- **Sunday:** Music-only; the DZMM live window is disabled.

The RJ FM music track is preloaded and fades up during the final eight seconds; it is at full level by 3:01 PM, when the DZMM window ends. Other program rotations and DZMM windows are defined in `lib/schedule.ts`. The station's regular off-air period is 3:00–4:00 AM Manila time.

## Evening DZMM window

- **Monday–Saturday, 10:00 PM:** The Love Connect station ident plays first. The DZMM live stream starts immediately after the 6.191-second clip ends, at about **10:00:06 PM**.
- The existing TV Patrol transition remains in place before the **6:30 PM** DZMM window. The overnight DZMM window ends at **3:00 AM** on weeknights and at midnight at the end of Saturday; Sunday remains music-only.
